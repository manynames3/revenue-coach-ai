# Revenue Use Cases

RevenueCoach AI is intentionally scoped to sales and revenue conversations, not general-purpose communication coaching. The product direction borrows proven patterns from AI roleplay, sales enablement, and revenue intelligence platforms while keeping the current demo honest about what is implemented today.

## Benchmarked Patterns

- Yoodli-style enablement: sales teams practice realistic conversations, receive structured feedback, reinforce skills over time, and let managers track readiness.
- AI sales roleplay platforms: reps practice buyer personas, discovery, cold calls, objections, demos, and methodology-specific scenarios before live customer conversations.
- Revenue intelligence tools: managers use call analytics to identify risk, soft-skill gaps, coaching opportunities, and deal execution issues.

## Current End-User Workflows

- Score real sales calls from transcripts or audio.
- Review a manager scorecard with discovery, objection handling, closing, follow-up, buying signals, and manager notes.
- Inspect question-led sales psychology feedback for trust, pain depth, urgency, decision clarity, money readiness, and resistance management.
- Use suggested better questions, objection psychology, coaching drills, and follow-up drafts to improve the next buyer interaction.
- Track team-level call volume, analyzed calls, average score, and the weakest recent skill category.

## Revenue-Focused Use Cases

### Discovery Depth

Help sellers move beyond surface pain into consequence, urgency, decision cost, and emotional driver. This is most valuable for high-ticket consultative selling where the buyer must understand the cost of staying the same before discussing price.

### Objection Handling

Coach reps to diagnose the objection behind price, timing, trust, authority, or status quo concerns before responding. The goal is lower resistance and clearer next steps, not better rebuttal scripts.

### Decision Clarity

Identify whether the buyer has a real decision path, clear urgency, budget logic, stakeholder alignment, and a meaningful next commitment.

### Follow-Up Rescue

Turn the call analysis into follow-up language that references the buyer's stated pain, blocker, and desired outcome instead of sending generic recap emails.

### Manager Coaching

Give managers a fast path from scorecard to next drill: pick the weakest skill, assign a focused practice motion, then compare the next analyzed call against the same rubric.

## Near-Term Product Direction

- Build AI buyer simulations from existing call patterns and sales psychology gaps.
- Add certification workflows for discovery, objection handling, demo, and closing readiness.
- Add rep trend lines so managers can see whether practice improves live-call behavior.
- Connect CRM fields such as stage, amount, close date, and outcome so coaching impact can be tied to pipeline movement.
- Add team playbooks for repeatable high-ticket sales motions.
